Interpretation

Lower statistical deviation generally indicates better concealment, but no steganographic method is guaranteed to evade steganalysis. A high PSNR and SSIM close to 1.0 indicate the stego image is visually near-identical to the cover — but a determined analyst using statistical tools (like the chi-square test above) may still be able to detect that something is hidden, even without being able to read it (that protection comes from the AES-256-GCM encryption layer, not the steganography itself).
