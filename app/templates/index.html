What is steganography?

Steganography hides the existence of a message, unlike encryption, which scrambles a message but still reveals that one exists. A stego image looks like an ordinary photo — nothing hints that data is hidden inside.

🔧

What is LSB embedding?

Every pixel channel (red, green, blue) is a byte from 0-255. Changing just its last bit shifts the value by at most 1 — invisible to the human eye, but enough space across millions of pixels to store real data.

🛡

How this app secures your files

Your file is encrypted with AES-256-GCM (confidentiality + integrity) before it ever touches the image. Even if someone suspects a stego image, they still need your password to read anything.

Why encryption + steganography together?

Encryption alone announces "something secret is here" — an encrypted blob is obviously not a normal file. Steganography alone can be brute-forced or statistically detected once someone knows to look. Combining both means an observer first has to notice the hidden channel, and then still has to break AES-256-GCM to read anything — two independent barriers instead of one.
